01

About this app

Script Manager and Downloader is a companion download-and-extract tool for a separate script browser app. When you choose a script there, it hands this app a link; this app downloads that file to your device and, if you ask it to, extracts it into a game's data folder. Downloading and extracting happen on your device. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which are described in section 5.

This app is an independent, unofficial tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with any third-party game or publisher.

02

Information we handle

We do not operate any servers, accounts, or backend for this app, so there is nothing for us — the developer — to collect. Everything below stays on your device:

Deep-link data. When the script browser app hands off a script, it includes a file URL, a file name, and up to two preview image URLs. This app keeps that information in memory only, to show you the download screen. It is never written to disk, logged, or sent to us.

Folder access. To list the .zip files in your downloads, Android asks you to grant access once through its standard folder picker. The resulting permission reference is saved only in this app's own private storage on your device, so it can remember your choice — it never leaves your device.

The app makes network requests to fetch what you asked for: the script file and preview images at the URLs the script browser app provided. Those requests go directly from your device to whichever server hosts that file, not through us. The ads in the app make their own requests to Google, which section 5 covers.

04

Shizuku & extraction

Extracting a downloaded script into a game's data folder requires broader file access than a normal app is given. For that one action, this app optionally uses Shizuku, a separate, open-source system tool you install and run yourself.

  • Shizuku is never required just to browse or download — only to extract a file.
  • You grant it permission explicitly, inside Shizuku's own prompt, each time it's needed.
  • The extraction command runs entirely on your device; no file contents or paths are sent anywhere.

05

Ads & third parties

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. A rewarded ad plays before a download starts, and a full-screen ad plays before a script is extracted. To show and measure these ads and to prevent ad fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app automatically collects the following and sends it to Google:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage
  • Device identifiers, namely your Android advertising ID and app set ID

This data is encrypted in transit. Google uses it for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, and may use your advertising ID to personalize the ads you see. How Google handles it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We, the developer, don't receive this data about you. AdMob only gives us totals, such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before it shows ads. You can change your choice at any time with the shield button at the top of the Home screen.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your phone's settings under Google → Ads (on some phones, Privacy → Ads).
  • Review how Google personalizes ads for you at My Ad Center.

Nothing else is shared

Apart from Google AdMob, we do not sell, rent, or share data with anyone. The app contains:

  • No other analytics or tracking software development kits (SDKs)
  • No crash-reporting services
  • No user accounts, logins, or profiles

06

Storage & retention

We do not keep any data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. Data collected by Google AdMob is kept by Google under its own retention policies. You can reset your advertising ID at any time so that future ads are no longer linked to past activity.

Downloaded scripts remain in your chosen download folder until you remove them yourself, and the saved folder-access reference is cleared automatically if you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.
